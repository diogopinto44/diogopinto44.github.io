The first months of my internship

In July 2023, I joined M N Ramos Ferreira Engenharia, SA as a marketing intern. I got straight to work managing the Group’s website and creating social media content.

I was also responsible for updating the Group’s visual identity at a time when the company was making significant progress in energy efficiency. The goal was to reflect that progress in its communications, in line with its sustainability policy. I refreshed the letterhead, business cards and email signature, and extended the same visual language to the corporate brochure and company presentation.

Sustainability also shaped one of my earliest challenges: during my first two months as an intern, I was responsible for registering the Project Earth by Grupo Ramos Ferreira brand. More than a visual identity, Project Earth is a Group initiative supported by a multidisciplinary team working on engineering solutions focused on sustainability and energy efficiency, from design through installation and maintenance. My contribution focused on branding and communication, connecting the brand with sustainability initiatives and content to give these topics a clearer, more recognisable presence. Explore Project Earth.

Alongside these responsibilities, I began working on the three-year Marketing plan by analysing the market and the competition. I gathered information on other companies in the sector, reviewed their strategies and identified opportunities to propose improvements and new approaches. This analysis also highlighted the need to establish a dedicated structure for the Group’s communication plan.

In April 2024, I became a Marketing Specialist. I work in marketing and communication for an international B2B group in engineering and specialist installations. My role covers digital channels, corporate content, editorial design, recruitment communications and events.

01

Strategy and planning

My strategic work began with market and competitor analysis, focusing on marketing and communication practices. Using these findings, I reviewed and implemented the 2023–2025 three-year Strategic Marketing Plan, proposing improvements and new initiatives. This assessment also led me to structure the Group’s Communications Plan, which had not previously been organised in the same formal way.

I subsequently contributed to the development of the 2026–2028 three-year Strategic Marketing Plan, as well as its review and implementation. Alongside the Communications Plan, I developed content strategies, defined indicators for monthly monitoring and contributed to the visual identity guidelines, in line with the brand’s refreshed look. The new plan carried forward priorities that required further work or reassessment.

  • Market research, competitor benchmarking and opportunity identification.
  • Review and implementation of the 2023–2025 three-year Strategic Marketing Plan.
  • Structuring the communication plan and content strategies.
  • Defining KPIs and monitoring performance indicators monthly.
  • Creating the visual identity guidelines manual to support the refreshed design.
  • Contribution to the development, review and implementation of the 2026–2028 plan.

Marketing plans, communication plans and other internal planning materials are not shared for confidentiality reasons.

02

Website and digital communication

When I took over the corporate website, I found disorganised content and missing information, an outdated project portfolio — with some projects lacking photographs and others using old images — and large files that increased load times on certain pages. Search engine optimisation also received little attention, particularly page titles and keywords, alongside minor configuration issues.

I started by reorganising the information and addressing configuration details. I then undertook a near-complete review of the project portfolio: ordering projects by completion date, refreshing their presentation, compressing images and introducing basic SEO practices. My goal was to make the website more up to date, better organised and more effective at showcasing the Group’s work. I also monitored its performance through Google Analytics 4.

Updating the imagery also required work beyond the website itself. I began by retrieving photographs that were already available on the internal server but had never been used. For projects without suitable visual records, I contacted project managers and coordinated the collection of materials with different teams. Later on, I also began taking some of the photographs myself. The portfolio overhaul therefore combined archive research and organisation, internal coordination and hands-on content production.

Performance monitoring. I tracked website performance monthly in Google Analytics 4, reviewing traffic patterns and identifying opportunities for improvement. Internal operational figures are not disclosed in this portfolio.

  • Restructuring a portfolio of more than 360 projects.
  • Publishing around 24 new projects, writing 21 news articles and creating two institutional pages.
Social media

From publishing to editorial strategy

When I took over the Group’s LinkedIn and Facebook channels, I built the content strategy from the ground up. I organised posts into categories and introduced an editorial calendar to plan publishing frequency and mark relevant dates.

I also worked on visual consistency: standardising the use of the logo across posts and adapting formats, dimensions and layouts to each platform’s requirements. At the same time, I started selecting content based on its quality and relevance, improving the copywriting and using topic-specific hashtags.

As the company looked to expand its presence to another social platform, I was also responsible for creating the Group’s Instagram account. This opened up another communication channel and gave us the opportunity to experiment with different publishing formats, including Stories.

Social media management became guided by clearer editorial criteria, combining content planning, brand consistency, copywriting and channel-specific communication.

Performance monitoring. I tracked audience growth, reach, impressions and interactions over time and reviewed content priorities using monthly reporting. Figures taken from internal reports are not disclosed here.

04

Recruitment communications and application management

My involvement in recruitment focuses on communicating vacancies and providing administrative support throughout the process. Once a hiring need has been identified and approved, I gather the information needed for the job listing, publish it on recruitment platforms and prepare social media content using an established visual template.

As applications come in, I receive and organise them in the Group’s internal portal, making them accessible to the HR team and the managers responsible for deciding which candidates progress to the next stages. My contribution therefore covers vacancy promotion and application organisation, rather than candidate selection decisions.

  • Preparation and promotion of more than 30 job opportunities.
  • Publication on job boards and communication across social media.
  • Receipt, organisation and logging of applications in an internal portal.
05

Events and social responsibility

I developed an Events Plan and, for internally organised initiatives, I usually coordinate almost the entire process: planning, logistics, delivery and communication before, during and after each event. I work with considerable autonomy, seeking management approval for specific decisions.

For external events, I coordinate the Group’s participation, support logistics and material preparation, and manage communication about the Group’s presence before, during and after the event. My role therefore varies by context, from direct event organisation to participation coordination and corporate communication.

Most of these events are coordinated remotely from Portugal, in collaboration with the teams involved. In 2025, I also travelled to Angola to attend and represent the Group in person at the 2nd Standardisation Forum for the Construction Sector in Angola and the iEngenharia BootCamp, complementing the preparation carried out in Portugal with on-site participation.

Featured project — 2023 Christmas Party. This was one of my first major event-management challenges at the company. I coordinated venue research and booking, decoration, menu and entertainment choices, seating arrangements and employee registrations. I also prepared communications before, during and after the event and produced a year-in-review video. Managing an event of this complexity so early in my time with the Group helped me develop the autonomy I would later bring to other events.

  • Direct organisation of six corporate events.
  • Coordination of two social responsibility initiatives.
  • Support in delivering six internal and industry events.
