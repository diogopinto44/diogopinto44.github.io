Chapter 01

Eight years
of scouting

From October 2016 to October 2024, I was part of Corpo Nacional de Escutas. My involvement in the movement helped me develop a strong connection to teamwork, leadership and the ability to take on new challenges.

Along the way, I helped organise national gatherings for 100 to 200 participants. I also joined the logistics preparation team for ACANAC 2022, an event attended by more than 25,000 scouts.

Chapter 02

Taking part.
Taking responsibility.

At Cenáculo do Núcleo Cidade do Porto, I helped organise three project cycles. I took on the general coordination of the 16th Cenáculo, leading a team of 16 and managing budgets and resources.

From September 2023 to September 2024, I was a member of CNE’s National Marketing Team. I contributed to goal setting, strategic planning and identifying potential sponsors and partners among companies and public bodies.

A personal discovery

“I learned that my future had always had more to do with numbers and creativity than with laws and judgments.”

Chapter 03

The plan was Law.
The path was Marketing.

My connection with marketing began in an unexpected way. When the time came to apply to university, I still did not really know what the field involved. For several years, I had imagined a different future: I wanted to become a judge.

I grew up around people who worked in law and, around seventh or eighth grade, began to see it as my own path. I felt more comfortable with languages, history and the humanities than with numbers and the exact sciences. Choosing Languages and Humanities at secondary school reflected those interests and my ambition to study Law.

Marketing did not even appear among my choices in the first round of university applications. My priorities were Law in Porto and Coimbra, followed by other subjects that interested me or remained connected to the legal field, such as Criminology and Solicitor Studies.

My national exam results fell short of my expectations and affected my initial applications. I retook the exams and improved significantly. I was offered a place in Tourism in Aveiro, but it did not feel like the right choice, so I decided to wait for the second-round results.

It was during that process that I discovered Marketing. It was a different field from the choices I had made up to that point, but its combination of analysis and creativity sparked my curiosity. Not yet knowing exactly what to expect was part of the appeal. Among further applications to Law, I was offered a place in Marketing at ISCAP.

I chose to stay. During my degree, I discovered that analysis and numbers could also be part of a field I enjoyed, especially when combined with creativity. Today, I have no regrets: I found a path I had not anticipated when I first started thinking about my future.
