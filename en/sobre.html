Prologue

Before the CV,
the person.

I’m Diogo. My journey brings together marketing, scouting and volunteering. They are different experiences, but each is part of who I am.

I enjoy following ideas from planning through to delivery. Through work and community involvement, I have found opportunities to do that as part of a team, take responsibility and keep learning.

Chapter 01

Eight years
of scouting

From October 2016 to October 2024, I was part of Corpo Nacional de Escutas. My involvement in the movement helped me develop a strong connection to teamwork, leadership and the ability to take on new challenges.

Along the way, I helped organise national gatherings for 100 to 200 participants. I also joined the logistics preparation team for ACANAC 2022, an event attended by more than 25,000 scouts.

Chapter 02

Taking part.
Taking responsibility.

At Cenáculo do Núcleo Cidade do Porto, I helped organise three project cycles. I took on the general coordination of the 16th Cenáculo, leading a team of 16 and managing budgets and resources.

From September 2023 to September 2024, I was a member of CNE’s National Marketing Team. I contributed to goal setting, strategic planning and identifying potential sponsors and partners among companies and public bodies.

Chapter 03

Building a path
in marketing

In September 2018, I started my bachelor’s degree in Marketing at ISCAP. My first professional experience in the field came in 2021 at GainStation, where I progressed from a curricular internship to a Marketing Specialist role.

In 2023, I joined Grupo Ramos Ferreira and began my master’s degree in Digital Marketing. I completed it in November 2025 with a final grade of 17/20, while developing my work in B2B communication and marketing.
